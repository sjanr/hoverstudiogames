Where it began

A hummingbird appeared on my balcony, 22 floors above Burnaby.

Soaked by rain, she kept coming back to the feeder I put out.

Did she still have a home to return to?

That question became this game.

Her neighbourhood is inspired by Edgemont Village in North Vancouver, my first home in Canada, where I saw my first hummingbird.

The game

The people here have moved too fast to tend their gardens.

The studio

Small lives. Vast worlds.

Hover Studio Games is an independent studio in Burnaby, B.C.

I'm Jan Rimar Suratos, founder and creative director. I trained as a ballet dancer and choreographer, and graduated in computer science from UBC Okanagan. My stories are shaped by dance and by film.

I make games about B.C., with B.C. artists, composers and specialists.

So that small lives like hers always have a home to return to.
